## what we offer

Empowering students to excel academically, grow personally, and lead with confidence.

###### Vision

Help kids realize their potential by providing a hassle free academic environment. Provide opportunities for children to go beyond the corners of the classroom and learn from hands on experiences. Present human values enriched learning atmosphere.

###### Mission

Provide quality uncompromised concept based teaching learning experience. Inculcate human centric value rich learning environment. Impart physical education & adventure activities to the ambitious students and help achieve their goals. Predominate the educational service to the rural students and uplift socio-economic standards

###### Learning Process

'Free kids' will access the latest and novel technologies and equip themselves to become the best.

###### Creative Lessons

NPPS believes in Facilitated Learning. Every learning session is supported with activity plans. Learning happens through experiential and inquisitive method. The learning outcome is the key component that is exercised in the formal classroom environment.

## About New Pratham Public School

NPPS is awarded the best CBSE SCHOOL for 3 consecutive years for OUTSTANDING ALL ROUND EXCELLENCE in Making education a Priority.

Newpratham Public School is established in the year 2010 and managed by the Sree Venkateshwara group of companies (IOCL Indiranagar Petrol Bunk) under the argis of sree Venkateshwara Charitable Trust.

The School is affiliated to the Central Board of Secondary Education(CBSE, New Delhi) and offersa Clases from Nursery to X with the dedicated vision of 'All Round Development' of each and every child in an ambient atmosphere of love and respect. We at NPPS endeavor to instill self-discipline and develop self-confidence in the children, with an aim to establish a conductive environment in which childrencan learn, play & grow to be true and responsible citizens of our Country. We impart sterling Education as Knowledge is the most powerful tool to create a better tomorrow.

AIMS towards Creating Future Leaders

NPPS aims at providing high-quality education at an affordable cost with the conceot of NO DONATION. Keeping in view, all the rules and regulations laid down by the National Education Policy NEP2020, the entire curriculum is mapped accordingly and the teachers are trained and eqipped to tutor in line with the NEP pedagogy.

New features are regularly being added to the campus to ensure holistic development of our students. Now we are equiped with 2 playgrounds, namely, NPPS MULTI-SPORTS ACTIVITY CENTRE, which is a state of the art playground, with a vast play area for our students and NPPS PLAYZONE which is a well-defined futuristic play area with exceptional playing equipment that will boost physical development, as we aim at bestowing professional training upon our future athelets.

NPPS is a hub of unique facilities, which includes Children Gymnasium, Cricket Bowling Machine, Indoor Sports Activity Centre, Mini Pool, A/C Class Rooms, well equipped Laboratories etc., While also endeavouring to instill self-discipline in the children and develop self-confidence inn them.

##### Pragmatic women leaders in education - award

Our managing trustee was honored with the pragmatic women leaders in education award in recognition of her visionary leadership, practical approach to problem-solving, and unwavering commitment to academic excellence. this prestigious accolade celebrates her impactful contributions in shaping a progressive learning environment, empowering educators, and fostering holistic development among students. her dedication continues to inspire innovation and excellence within the educational community.

  

![Excellent educonnectln - award](http://www.newprathampublicschool.in/images/achievements/8p0388wg68.jpeg)

##### Excellent educonnectln - award

Our school is proud to announce that it has been conferred an “excellent” rating by educonnectln, standing out among 6,775 schools across bengaluru. this prestigious recognition reflects our unwavering commitment to academic excellence, holistic development, and quality education. the educonnectln evaluation process assessed schools on multiple parameters, including teaching–learning practices, student engagement, infrastructure, innovation, and overall institutional effectiveness. achieving an excellent rating in such a comprehensive and competitive assessment is a significant milestone for our school.

  

![Victory quantum - inter school sports meet](http://www.newprathampublicschool.in/images/achievements/hjvlqr5rt.jpg)

##### Victory quantum - inter school sports meet

Our school proudly hosted the inter-school sports meet, bringing together young athletes from various schools to celebrate sportsmanship, teamwork, and healthy competition. the event provided a vibrant platform for students to showcase their athletic skills while fostering friendship and mutual respect among participating schools.adding to the pride of the occasion, our school’s kho kho girls team delivered an outstanding performance and emerged as the winners, earning well-deserved applause and admiration from all.

  

![Abacus - a celebration of mental math excellence](http://www.newprathampublicschool.in/images/achievements/g8w3t5il4q.jpg)

##### Abacus - a celebration of mental math excellence

An exciting interschool abacus competition was recently hosted at adharsh international school, hoskote, bringing together young math enthusiasts from various schools to showcase their remarkable mental calculation skills. the event witnessed enthusiastic participation from students who demonstrated exceptional speed, accuracy, and concentration.

  

![India k-12 awards (ika) - 2025](http://www.newprathampublicschool.in/images/achievements/skym7891or.jpeg)

##### India k-12 awards (ika) - 2025

Celebrating excellence: our school receives the k–12 award we are proud to announce that our school has been honored with the prestigious k–12 award, a recognition of our unwavering commitment to academic excellence and holistic education. this achievement reflects the collective efforts of our dedicated educators, enthusiastic students, and supportive parent community.

  

![The power play- inter school competition](http://www.newprathampublicschool.in/images/achievements/9evouos6vg.)

##### The power play- inter school competition

In the arena of sports, our kho-kho girls team delivered an outstanding performance at the interschool sports competition held at christ international school, bengaluru. displaying exceptional teamwork, agility, and sportsmanship, the team emerged champions, securing the first place in kho-kho. their victory stands as a testament to disciplined training, perseverance, and the indomitable spirit of our young athletes.
